## Bring your chats with you

Open the chat history. Next to your Gisoo chats you see the chats this folder has in Claude Code, Codex, GitHub Copilot, Gemini CLI, Cline, Roo Code and Kilo Code. Search by title or by any word in the messages, pick one and continue it in Gisoo.
